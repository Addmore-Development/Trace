How it works

Up and running in four simple steps

  1. 01

    Sign up

    Create your free account and invite your team. Everyone gets their own login and role.

  2. 02

    Add assets

    Log each asset with its name, category, location, price, supplier and a photo. Addmore gives it a unique tag number.

  3. 03

    Print and stick tags

    Print your QR code tags, such as ADM-LT-0042, and stick one on each item.

  4. 04

    Scan and track

    Scan with a phone to see the asset, assign it, update its status or log a repair.
